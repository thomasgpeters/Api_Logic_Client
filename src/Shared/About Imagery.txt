# Imagery Business Systems, LLC
We build business software at Imagery Business Systems, and a growing set of tools for FutureBasic programmers on the Mac. This app is one of them: a free module, with an example that shows you how to use it.

## Free modules for FutureBasic (MIT licence)
- **SQL Generator**: describe a SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE or DELETE as a dictionary (its tables, columns, filters and joins) and get safe, parameterized SQL for SQLite. Names are quoted for you, and every value is bound rather than pasted into the SQL.
- **LogicBank for FutureBasic**: business rules for your data, declared once and enforced on every save. Sums, counts, formulas, copies, constraints, parent checks and delete actions stay in step on their own as rows change. It was inspired by LogicBank for API Logic Server and rewritten for FutureBasic.
- **Web Toolkit for FutureBasic**: web applications written in FutureBasic. You write the widgets and the functions their events call, and the page works in any browser, behind nginx. You can lay pages out in XIB files with the Layout Tool.
All three are yours to download, use and change for free, with their examples and documentation, from the website below.

## App Builder for FutureBasic
A Mac app that builds a complete FutureBasic application from your database: the windows and forms (XIB), the lists, the data binding and the business rules, ready for you to open in the FutureBasic editor and build.
- **Lite** is free when you register. You start from a SQLite database, and the app and its rules run on your Mac, with LogicBank for FutureBasic.
- **Enterprise** is licensed. It works in three tiers with API Logic Server: many users, the rules enforced on the server, sign-in and security, and rules shared with your Python project.
- We're also working on an AI assistant, though it isn't ready yet. You'll describe a rule in plain language, in your model's own names, and App Builder will turn it into the rule.

## Sample applications
We built these to show what the rules can do. Each comes with its database, its rules and its tests:
- **Telecom**: a mobile carrier's travel plans, priced from its roaming agreements.
- **Customs**: automated customs clearance, set in Bermuda, covering duty, risk lanes, release and the port gate.
- **Flight Planner**: VFR flight planning with wind, weight and balance, runways, weather and a go / no-go decision.
