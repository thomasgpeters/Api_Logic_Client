# Imagery Business Systems, LLC
Imagery Business Systems builds business software, and a growing set of tools for FutureBasic programmers on the Mac. This app is one of them: a free module, with an example that shows how to use it.

## Free modules for FutureBasic (MIT licence)
- **SQL Generator**: describe a SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE or DELETE as a dictionary - tables, columns, filters, joins - and generate safe, parameterized SQL for SQLite. Names are quoted for you, and every value is bound, never pasted into the SQL.
- **LogicBank for FutureBasic**: business rules for your data, declared once and enforced on every save - sums, counts, formulas, copies, constraints, parent checks and delete actions, kept in step automatically as rows change. Inspired by LogicBank for API Logic Server, rewritten for FutureBasic.
Both are free to download, use and change, with their examples and documentation, from the website below.

## App Builder for FutureBasic
A Mac app that builds a complete FutureBasic application from your database: the windows and forms (XIB), the lists, the data binding, and the business rules - ready to open in the FutureBasic editor and build.
- **Lite**: free with registration. Start from a SQLite database; the app and its rules run on your Mac, with LogicBank for FutureBasic.
- **Enterprise**: licensed. Three tiers with API Logic Server: many users, the rules enforced on the server, sign-in and security, rules shared with your Python project.
- An AI assistant (coming): describe a rule in plain language, in your model's own names, and App Builder turns it into the rule.

## Sample applications
Built to show what the rules can do, each with its database, its rules and its tests:
- **Telecom**: a mobile carrier's travel plans, priced from its roaming agreements.
- **Customs**: automated customs clearance, set in Bermuda - duty, risk lanes, release and the port gate.
- **Flight Planner**: VFR flight planning - wind, weight and balance, runways, weather and a go / no-go.
